How to use the portal

01Your detailsName, firm, email and phone. These go on the submission to CredX. 02BorrowerType, purpose and exit, with exit detail. Tick the occupancy declaration: CredX only does unregulated lending. 03SecuritiesAdd each property. LTV uses the lower of purchase price and market value. Add first charge details for any 2nd charge. 04Structure the dealEnter the net advance and term, or press Maximise loan for the most available at 70% LTV. 05Check the quoteThe Indicative Quote updates as you type. Criteria show Pass, Refer (orange) or Flag (red). 06Submit to CredXAdd commentary (required if anything is outside criteria) and press Submit to open a pre-filled email. 07Client formsDownload the Application Form and Statement of Assets and Liabilities for your client to complete.
